### A reference-level director's brief

**Camera:** Editorial moves only. Use pan/crop/tilt to *follow actual interaction*, not slowly zoom random screenshots. Motion vector layers should line up with real DOM geometry where possible.

**Compositions:** Full-frame, uncluttered, native 1920×1080. Live UI fills the frame. Use physically plausible document/card transitions and single-focus highlights. No 720p upscaling. No undirected neon, 3D coins, generic grids, gaudy glows, or random motion graphics unrelated to an action.

**Typography:** One prominent idea per frame, cream/black contrast, a single restrained copper accent; generous margins; no text that a judge cannot read on an X video on a phone. Subtitle lines must not obscure button labels or edits.

**Audio:** Record a completely new natural, confident 145–160-word script. Arrange narration into intentional lines **before** animating. Every scene corresponds to a spoken claim. Explicitly listen end-to-end for robotic phrasing, clipped joins, dead air, and words stretched across visual cuts. Original/licensed subtle music and sparse meaningful sound design. Never recycle the first cut's audio.

**Edit and encoding:** Fast meaning-first sequencing with visual variety, but not frenetic: a consequential change every ~2–4 seconds. H.264/AAC 1920×1080/30 fps, sharp UI typography and appropriate controlled quality (e.g. CRF 17–19 or visually confirmed ~10Mbps+ when necessary). Render a full MP4, 30–40 subtitle cues, thumbnail, genuine X-ready stills, video source/shot list and QA record. Film must be watched start-to-end **with and without audio** and on phone-size viewing.

**Professional independent review:** 25 points for clear causal user workflow, 25 for cinematic motion/art direction, 20 for actual complete PACT product representation, 15 for narration/audio and subtitles, 10 for truthful outcomes, 5 for file/export quality.
